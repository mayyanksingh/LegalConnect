Police Procedures

Understanding Regular FIR vs Zero FIR

A Zero FIR allows any police station to register a cognizable criminal complaint regardless of geographical jurisdiction, ensuring immediate preliminary action.

  • Zero FIR registered without a serial number at any station
  • Transferred subsequently to the jurisdictional police station
  • Essential for emergencies involving serious bodily harm
Free Legal Aid

Accessing Free Legal Aid via NALSA / DLSA

Article 39A of the Constitution guarantees free legal assistance to disadvantaged sections. Learn about eligibility criteria under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act.

  • Free for women, children, undertrial prisoners, and marginalized communities
  • Available in every district via the District Court Legal Services Committee
  • Lok Adalat alternative dispute resolution without court fees
